About

From requirements to working systems.

I’m Sean Syue, based in Taipei. My work spans AI product projects, business process automation and independently developed software. The résumé has the dates and responsibilities.

Background

I trained as a biologist and spent two years at a biotech company in Taipei, first as the person who kept twenty-odd projects moving, then as project manager for Prometheus Lab AI, an AI product that reads laboratory work through smart glasses. That product reached the Final Round of the Qualcomm Innovate in Taiwan Challenge 2025; my job was the requirements, the schedule, the partners across three countries, and the pitch — in Chinese, English and Japanese.

  • Research training: start from evidence, break a problem down, test the assumption
  • Product and project work: requirements, risk, cross-team and cross-company coordination
  • Automation on the job: n8n workflows across Ragic, Outlook and Excel; an AI content programme for ten-plus websites
  • Proposals to SBIR, the National Science and Technology Council and AAMA; booth duty at SusHi Tech Tokyo

Why I started building systems

Because most of the problems I was managing weren't schedule problems. They were process problems — and a process can be redesigned. So I stopped only chasing progress and started rebuilding the steps: automating the reminders and reconciliations first, then the content pipelines, and eventually whole systems.

Building them myself changed how I write requirements. I now know what a technical team runs into when it implements, tests and maintains something — so I define the problem before the feature, break vague requests into flows, data structures and checkable work items, and when I'm not sure a plan will hold up, I build a small prototype so the team can judge the trade-offs with something real in front of them.

The tool list — Claude Code, Codex, local models, n8n, Python, SQLite — matters less than that habit.
