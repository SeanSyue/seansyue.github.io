A shift in how I work

When a process keeps getting stuck,
I want to understand its structure.

On the job, I started connecting Ragic, Outlook, Excel and public APIs through n8n. The work included administration, AI projects and marketing. It taught me to look at the information and decisions between steps, and to make those handoffs explicit.

I took a career transition period from November 2025 to June 2026 to reassess my direction and prepare for my next role. Enterprise CRM, KYC and case-management work followed in July–August 2026.

Lately, I’ve been developing independent software and creative AI workflows. These give me a way to test an idea through its details: a responsibility model in AICC, access rules in Tetherlock, or the feel of a conversation in Heart Apartment.

Building prototypes has made my requirements more concrete. I can put a workflow, a data structure or a small experiment in front of people, so the next decision has something tangible behind it.

Working principles

What I pay attention to.

01

Give the requirement a shape

Write down what the result must do, what can vary and how someone will judge it. A useful specification leaves fewer decisions hidden.

Turning art direction into a visual specification →
02

Make trade-offs tangible

Compare a focused set of options in the setting where they will be used. Response time and interaction quality matter when someone is waiting for a character to reply.

Choosing a local model for the game →
03

Be clear about responsibility

Define who produces the work, who reviews it and which decisions need a person. The handoff belongs in the design.

04

Let the result change the method

A check earns its place by being useful. When a music-ranking script failed to predict my listening choices, I kept the findings and changed the process.

Working with AI

My decisions. AI-assisted implementation.

AI writes most of the code in my independent software projects. I define the problem, the architecture, the rules and acceptance criteria; direct the implementation; and review the results.

In the creative work, I set the visual or musical direction, compare generated versions and choose what fits the experience. Images and music are generated material shaped by that process.

The cases distinguish my contribution, what was tested and where the limits remain. I want readers to be able to follow the decisions, including the ones that did not work.
