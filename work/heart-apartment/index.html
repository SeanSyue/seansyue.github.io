Heart Apartment 心動公寓

A cozy life-sim for Windows about two roommates you can simply watch, or step into at the moments they hesitate — a small “life movie” that keeps going after each choice.

15-second showcase with the game's own title theme. Recorded 2026-09-22 from the current build.

Challenge

Characters need room to act and remember, while player choices remain meaningful. AI suggestions must still obey the game's current state and rules.

My contribution

I set the concept and art direction, selected the soundtrack, and playtested builds to define acceptance rules. AI collaborators implemented code, art, and story integration under that direction.

Evidence

See the recorded gameplay, the rules that check AI proposals, and the playtest that led to the no-face-overlap rule.

Game & AI design

How the characters think

01

The model proposes, the rules decide

A real rejection: the model proposed putting on an album and making tea. The game refused — “the character's state has changed; this old proposal is void.”

02

Seen vs told

Each story moment writes one memory line for the person who was there and a different one for the roommate who only heard about it. Later scenes check which.

03

Depth before breadth

Five tests per character: a reason, a contradiction, continuity, a difference — from her roommate and from herself when tired — and visible to the player.

Choosing the model

Qwen3-4B (Q5)median reply
0.53 s
Qwen3.5-9B (Q5)median reply
3.26 s

Current prompt, 8 scenarios × 2 seeds = 16 runs each, 2026-09-11. 95th percentile: 0.66 s vs 4.18 s. Measured on the target PC (i9-13900K, RTX 4070 Ti with 12 GB): the 4B ran fully on the GPU; the 9B did not fit, so 20 of its layers ran on the GPU and the rest on the CPU.

  • In an exploratory check across the same eight scenarios, the 9B made the same kind of mistake as the 4B and showed no clear quality advantage, so the game ships the 4B
  • Replies are labelled “AI reply”; if the model takes over 6 s or isn't installed, a hand-written line is used, labelled “text fallback”
  • A late answer never pops up after the moment has passed; the launcher only stops servers it started

One rule from a playtest: nothing may cover a face

  • What I saw — captions and panels sitting on the characters' faces in a recorded build (2026-09-15)
  • What changed — a hard rule for every caption, thought cloud, panel and HUD card, side characters included
  • How it's checked — recordings flag any frame where something covers a face; the 2026-09-22 recording logged zero
